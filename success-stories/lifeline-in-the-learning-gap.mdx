When the COVID-19 pandemic disrupted life in 2020, the shutdown of schools across Nigeria hit students from low-income communities the hardest. While some private schools transitioned online, many public schools—especially in under-resourced areas—offered no learning alternatives.

<Pullquote position="right">
  Students were on the verge of being left behind entirely.
</Pullquote>

The gap was growing, and students like Imole risked losing more than just class time. **That's when LOTSI stepped in.**

### The Continuous Learning Program

With movement restrictions gradually easing, we launched the Continuous Learning Program in Lekki—a makeshift academic hub for students locked out of school. Thanks to the generosity of **Faith Word Family Center**, we secured a space where volunteer teachers—both online and in person—could support students in core subjects like Mathematics and English.

The initiative gave students more than just academic support; it provided them with:

- **A safe and structured place to study** during uncertain times
- **Consistent learning routines** when everything else felt chaotic
- **Hope** that their education could continue despite the challenges

### Meeting Imole

Imole was one of the first students to join. Quiet but determined, he showed up consistently. Day after day, week after week, he came ready to learn. With guidance and support from our volunteer teachers, he began to recover the lost ground academically.

His dedication paid off when he eventually sat for his WAEC exams—**and passed**.

### The Transformation Continues

That one opportunity rekindled his commitment to learning. But Imole's story didn't end with passing his exams. Today, by God's grace and the continued support of sponsors including **Deeper Life Church**, Imole has achieved something remarkable:

**He graduated from Covenant University with a degree in Business Administration.**

<Quote>
  "If I didn't have that space to keep learning during COVID, I don't know if
  I'd be where I am today."
</Quote>

### What This Means

<Pullquote position="center" size="large">
  When young people receive structure, support, and belief at their point of
  need—they don't just survive. They thrive.
</Pullquote>

Imole's journey from a struggling student during the pandemic to a university graduate proves this powerful truth.

His transformation reminds us that:

- **Academic support during crisis** can change the trajectory of a life
- **Community partnerships** amplify our impact
- **Consistent encouragement** helps students push through challenges
- **Investment in education** yields returns that last generations
