## The Challenge

Divine was one of many young people caught in the harsh reality of Nigeria's economic climate. Having been out of school for four years, his formal education had been put on hold, limiting his future prospects. While he had found an interest in fashion design, the immediate pressures of survival often overshadow long-term investments like education for families struggling financially.

His mother, facing desperate circumstances and lacking the vision to see beyond immediate financial needs, was unconvinced about the inherent value of education. For her, the priority was immediate income, not a prolonged investment in schooling that offered no quick returns. This mindset, though understandable given the economic hardship, presented a significant barrier to Divine's progress.

## LOTSI's Intervention

When LOTSI encountered Divine, we recognized his potential and the critical need for intervention. Our comprehensive approach aimed to remove as many barriers as possible for his family. We immediately enrolled Divine in a private school, providing him with a structured learning environment tailored to help him catch up. Furthermore, we extended our support to his three younger siblings, placing them in government schools to ensure they also had access to education.

Understanding the family's financial strain, LOTSI went a step further by covering 90% of their family's rent. This significant financial relief was intended to alleviate immediate pressures and create a more stable home environment, allowing the children to focus on their studies without the constant threat of eviction.

<Pullquote position="left" size="small">
  Our comprehensive support aimed to remove as many barriers as possible.
</Pullquote>

Despite this comprehensive support, Divine's mother remained unconvinced about the long-term value of education. Her desperation led her to pressure Divine to abandon school and find work, emphasizing the need for immediate income. Tragically, she also pulled the younger siblings from school and sent them back to live in the village, severing their access to the education LOTSI had facilitated.

## The Transformation

In the face of his mother's doubts and the profound disruption to his siblings' education, Divine possessed something truly remarkable: an unwavering determination. His commitment to his educational journey did not waver. Even as immediate financial pressures mounted and his family's situation changed, Divine held firm to his belief in the power of learning.

<Quote author="Divine">
  "I know that staying in school is my best chance for a better future, no
  matter how hard it gets."
</Quote>

LOTSI continues to stand by Divine. We recognize his incredible resilience and persistence against such formidable odds. Our support extends beyond tuition, sometimes even helping with feeding costs to ensure he has the basic necessities to continue his perseverance in school. This ongoing commitment is crucial in reinforcing his resolve.

## Current Status

Divine's story is still being written, a testament to his incredible spirit and the ongoing challenge of transforming mindsets amidst hardship. His determination, however, gives us immense hope. He continues his educational journey, fueled by an internal drive that transcends immediate circumstances.
